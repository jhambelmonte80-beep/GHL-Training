How to use this lab

For every client request, follow this order: Clarify → Inspect → Fix → Test → Report. Never change a live client's settings until you understand the expected result, have permission and know how to reverse the change.

The workbench is a fictional training simulator. Its switches and test buttons do not connect to a real GHL account, send messages, spend ad budget or change real contacts.
